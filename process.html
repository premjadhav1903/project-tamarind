A RESPONSIBLE FOUNDATION

Build the process before making the promise.

Tamarind processing requires careful decisions about raw material selection, cleaning, extraction, filtration, packaging, storage and food-safety controls. The correct method depends on the final product and validated production design.

We will avoid unsupported claims and share information only when it reflects our actual operations.

01

Raw material

Define sourcing criteria and incoming inspection for tamarind appropriate to the product.

02

Process design

Develop and validate a suitable cleaning, extraction and handling process.

03

Quality controls

Establish relevant testing, traceability, packaging and storage requirements before sale.

These are intended development principles, not a claim that a production facility, certification or validated process currently exists.
